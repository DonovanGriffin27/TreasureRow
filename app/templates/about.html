The Vault

Fashion Marketplace

About

The Vault is a campus marketplace built for Hampton University students. Open a storefront, list clothing you want to sell, and browse what other students on campus have up for grabs — all without leaving your Hampton email behind.

How it works

Create an account with your Hampton email, open a personal storefront, and list items with sizes, quantities, and fulfillment details. Buyers can browse storefronts, add items to a cart, check out, and request a return if something doesn't work out.

Who it's for

Any Hampton University student looking to buy, sell, or trade clothing with people on campus instead of strangers online.

Questions or issues

Reach out to a seller directly through their storefront for order questions, or email TheVaultAdmins@my.hamptonu.edu for anything else.
